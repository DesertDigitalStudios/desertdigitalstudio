Audit Score: 73/100 — Good business, outdated website experience

Key Issues Found

Trust & Security

  • The secure version of the site throws an HTTPS / certificate error.
  • That creates an immediate trust problem for new visitors.

Outdated First Impression

  • The site looks extremely dated and uses an old “click to enter” style flow.
  • It does not reflect the quality or personality of the business.

Weak Mobile Experience

  • Tourists are likely visiting from their phones while already in town.
  • The current site does not feel built for quick mobile decisions.

Missed Conversion Opportunities

  • No strong modern call-to-action for directions, calling, or viewing packages.
  • Limited guidance for visitors deciding whether to stop in.

Why This Matters

For a Tombstone business like Can-Can, the website should help tourists make a fast decision: this looks fun, this looks real, and this is worth stopping in for. Right now, the business has the personality — but the site does not do enough to support it.

Recommended Website Upgrade

  • Modern, mobile-friendly site design
  • Working secure HTTPS setup
  • Strong homepage with old-time photo visuals
  • Clear hours, location, phone, and contact info
  • Simple photo gallery showing sample portraits
  • Clear call-to-action buttons for directions and contact
  • Optional package / experience info for tourists and families
  • Cleaner overall presentation while preserving the business’s western character

Project Goal

The goal is not to change the personality of the business. The goal is to preserve that character while giving Can-Can a cleaner, more trustworthy, more usable online presence.

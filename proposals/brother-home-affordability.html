Best Overall Recommendation

Because vehicle debt is already fairly heavy, the safest target is probably in the $210,000–$240,000 range. That keeps the monthly payment more manageable and leaves more room for groceries, gas, utilities, repairs, and savings.

Important Reminder

The down payment grant helps him get into the home, but it does not change what feels comfortable month to month. The smart move is to avoid shopping only by maximum lender approval.

Next Step

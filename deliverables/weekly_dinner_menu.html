Weekly Dinner Menu (Monday-Friday)

Quick, budget-conscious dinners focused on protein, vegetables, and moderate to minimal carbs.

Monday - Garlic Chicken and Broccoli Skillet

Time: 20-25 minutes
Protein: High
Carbs: Low

Ingredients

  • 1.5 to 2 lbs chicken thighs or chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 2 heads broccoli or 2 bags frozen broccoli
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 to 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 to 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: cooked rice for a small side portion

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Season chicken with salt, pepper, and paprika.
  3. Cook chicken for 6 to 8 minutes until browned and cooked through.
  4. Remove chicken and set aside if the pan is crowded.
  5. Add onion and broccoli to the skillet. Cook 4 to 5 minutes.
  6. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds more.
  7. Return chicken to the pan and stir everything together.
  8. Serve as is, or with a small portion of rice.

Tuesday - Ground Turkey Taco Bowls

Time: 20 minutes
Protein: High
Carbs: Low to moderate

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs ground turkey
  • 1 packet taco seasoning or homemade taco seasoning
  • 1 head romaine or iceberg lettuce, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • Salsa
  • Optional: black beans, shredded cheese, avocado

Instructions

  1. Heat a skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add ground turkey and cook until browned, about 7 to 8 minutes.
  3. Drain excess liquid if needed.
  4. Add taco seasoning and a splash of water. Stir until coated.
  5. In bowls, layer lettuce, tomatoes, onion, and bell pepper.
  6. Add the turkey on top.
  7. Finish with salsa and optional toppings.
  8. Add a small spoon of black beans if you want moderate carbs.

Wednesday - Beef and Zucchini Stir-Fry

Time: 20-25 minutes
Protein: High
Carbs: Low

Ingredients

  • 1 to 1.5 lbs ground beef or thin sliced beef
  • 2 zucchini, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables
  • 2 to 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add beef and cook until browned.
  3. Add onion and cook 2 minutes.
  4. Add zucchini and frozen vegetables.
  5. Stir-fry for 5 to 6 minutes until vegetables are tender.
  6. Add garlic, soy sauce, and black pepper.
  7. Stir for 1 to 2 more minutes.
  8. Serve hot.

Thursday - Egg Roll in a Bowl

Time: 20 minutes
Protein: High
Carbs: Very low

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs ground pork or ground turkey
  • 1 bag coleslaw mix
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil, optional
  • Green onions, optional
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add ground pork or turkey and cook until browned.
  3. Add onion and cook for 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Add garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
  5. Add coleslaw mix and cook until softened, about 4 to 5 minutes.
  6. Add soy sauce and sesame oil if using.
  7. Stir well and cook another 1 to 2 minutes.
  8. Top with green onions if desired.

Friday - Sheet Pan Sausage and Vegetables

Time: 25-30 minutes
Protein: Moderate to high
Carbs: Low

Ingredients

  • 1 package smoked sausage or chicken sausage, sliced
  • 2 zucchini, chopped
  • 1 head broccoli or frozen broccoli
  • 2 bell peppers, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 to 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Put sausage and vegetables on a sheet pan.
  3. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  4. Toss everything together on the pan.
  5. Roast for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring halfway through.
  6. Cook until vegetables are tender and sausage is browned.
  7. Serve immediately.

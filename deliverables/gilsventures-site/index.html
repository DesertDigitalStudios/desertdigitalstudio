About Gil's Ventures

Generations of exceptional craftsmanship from an El Paso-based family company

Carlos Gil began his roofing career in 1981, working for and leading crews in his father's company. After decades roofing homes and buildings across El Paso, he launched Gil's Ventures and built a company known for dependable workmanship across residential, commercial, military, state, and federal projects.

Today, the company continues to offer high-quality roofing systems, restoration work, and custom sheet metal solutions backed by practical experience in the field.

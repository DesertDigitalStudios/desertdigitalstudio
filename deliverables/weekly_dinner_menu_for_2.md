# Weekly Dinner Menu for 2 (Monday-Friday)

Quick, budget-conscious dinners for **2 adults**, focused on **high protein**, **vegetables**, and **moderate to minimal carbs**.

---

## Monday - Garlic Chicken and Broccoli Skillet
**Serves:** 2  
**Time:** 20-25 minutes  
**Protein:** High  
**Carbs:** Low

### Ingredients
- 1 lb chicken thighs or chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 large head broccoli or 1 bag frozen broccoli
- 1/2 onion, sliced
- 2 to 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: 1 cup cooked rice total if you want a small carb side

### Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Season chicken with salt, pepper, and paprika.
3. Cook chicken for 6 to 8 minutes until browned and cooked through.
4. Remove chicken if needed so the vegetables cook evenly.
5. Add onion and broccoli to the skillet. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes.
6. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
7. Return chicken to the skillet, stir together, and serve.

## Tuesday - Ground Turkey Taco Bowls
**Serves:** 2  
**Time:** 20 minutes  
**Protein:** High  
**Carbs:** Low to moderate

### Ingredients
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 2 tsp taco seasoning or to taste
- 1/2 head romaine or iceberg lettuce, chopped
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- Salsa
- Optional: 1/2 cup black beans, shredded cheese, avocado

### Instructions
1. Heat a skillet over medium heat.
2. Add ground turkey and cook until browned, about 7 to 8 minutes.
3. Drain any excess liquid if needed.
4. Add taco seasoning and a splash of water. Stir well.
5. Divide lettuce, tomato, onion, and bell pepper between 2 bowls.
6. Top with seasoned turkey.
7. Finish with salsa and any optional toppings.

## Wednesday - Beef and Zucchini Stir-Fry
**Serves:** 2  
**Time:** 20-25 minutes  
**Protein:** High  
**Carbs:** Low

### Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef or thin sliced beef
- 2 zucchini, sliced
- 1/2 onion, sliced
- 1 to 1.5 cups frozen mixed vegetables
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oil
- Black pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add beef and cook until browned.
3. Add onion and cook for 2 minutes.
4. Add zucchini and frozen vegetables.
5. Stir-fry for 5 to 6 minutes until vegetables are tender.
6. Add garlic, soy sauce, and black pepper.
7. Stir for 1 to 2 more minutes and serve.

## Thursday - Egg Roll in a Bowl
**Serves:** 2  
**Time:** 20 minutes  
**Protein:** High  
**Carbs:** Very low

### Ingredients
- 1 lb ground pork or ground turkey
- 1/2 bag coleslaw mix
- 1/2 onion, thinly sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil, optional
- Green onions, optional
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add ground pork or turkey and cook until browned.
3. Add onion and cook for 2 to 3 minutes.
4. Add garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
5. Add coleslaw mix and cook 4 to 5 minutes until softened.
6. Add soy sauce and sesame oil if using.
7. Stir well and cook another 1 to 2 minutes.
8. Top with green onions if desired.

---

## Friday - Sheet Pan Sausage and Vegetables
**Serves:** 2  
**Time:** 25-30 minutes  
**Protein:** Moderate to high  
**Carbs:** Low

### Ingredients
- 12 to 14 oz smoked sausage or chicken sausage, sliced
- 2 zucchini, chopped
- 1 head broccoli or 1 bag frozen broccoli
- 1 bell pepper, chopped
- 1/2 onion, chopped
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. Add sausage and vegetables to a sheet pan.
3. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
4. Toss everything together.
5. Roast for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring halfway through.
6. Cook until vegetables are tender and sausage is browned.
7. Serve hot.

# Budget Notes
To keep the cost down for 2 people:
- Use **chicken thighs** instead of chicken breast
- Buy **frozen broccoli** if it is cheaper
- Use **ground turkey** in multiple meals
- Skip expensive toppings unless you already have them
- Use leftover onion, garlic, and peppers across multiple dinners

---

# Optional Leftover Strategy
If you want a little extra for lunch the next day:
- Increase the Monday chicken to **1.25 lbs**
- Increase Tuesday turkey to **1.25 lbs**
- Make extra Thursday egg roll in a bowl, since it reheats well

That gives you a couple of easy leftover lunches without raising the grocery bill too much.

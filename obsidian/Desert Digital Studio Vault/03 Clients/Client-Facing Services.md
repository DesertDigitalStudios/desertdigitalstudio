# Client-Facing Services

Use this note as a clean internal draft for how DDS explains its offers to prospects.

## Positioning
Desert Digital Studio builds clean, modern websites for small businesses that want to look trustworthy, feel current, and make it easier for customers to get in touch.

## Core offers

### Launch Pad Website — $1200
- Timeline: 5–7 business days
- Best fit: A clean first website that makes the business look real, reachable, and current.

### Website Refresh — $900
- Timeline: 4–6 business days
- Best fit: A tighter, more trustworthy site that converts better without overcomplicating the build.

### Quick Wins Tune-Up — $500
- Timeline: 2–3 business days
- Best fit: A short project focused on the highest-impact fixes instead of a full rebuild.

## Standard inclusions
- Mobile-friendly build
- Clear calls to action and contact path
- Better trust and presentation than a bare Facebook page or outdated site
- Basic SEO foundations
- Google Analytics (GA4) added as a standard deliverable in the client’s account, with DDS retaining read access when possible

## Optional positioning angles
- New businesses that need a real website fast
- Existing businesses with an outdated or low-trust site
- Small businesses relying too heavily on Facebook or listings

## Tone
- Clear, local, and non-corporate
- Sell trust, clarity, and direct communication
- Do not overcomplicate the offer with jargon

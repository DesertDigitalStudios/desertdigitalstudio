# Desert Digital Studio Vault

This Obsidian vault is for DDS knowledge, notes, research, and operating docs.

## Folder guide
- `00 Inbox` — rough notes, quick captures, scraps
- `01 Dashboard` — top-level status notes and weekly planning
- `02 Leads` — lead research and business-specific notes
- `03 Clients` — active/past client notes and delivery context
- `04 Outreach` — email/DM scripts, follow-up ideas, objections
- `05 SOPs` — repeatable processes and checklists
- `06 Content Ideas` — post ideas, hooks, captions, content plans
- `07 Case Studies` — wins, before/after notes, proof
- `08 Local Markets` — city-by-city notes and business intel
- `09 Meetings` — meeting prep and recap notes
- `10 Templates` — reusable note templates
- `11 Assets` — links and notes for brand assets, not large binaries

## House rule
Use this vault for human-readable business memory.
Use the DDS dashboard/CRM for live operational state.

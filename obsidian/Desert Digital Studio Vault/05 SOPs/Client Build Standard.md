# Client Build Standard

## Purpose
Keep every DDS client project aligned with the current delivery standard.

## Minimum standard
- Mobile-friendly design
- Clear contact path
- Trust-focused copy/layout
- Basic SEO foundations
- Fast, simple user experience
- GA4 added as a standard deliverable in the client account when possible

## Delivery notes
- Favor clean and direct over flashy and overbuilt.
- Make the business look trustworthy and current.
- Keep the process easy for the client to understand.

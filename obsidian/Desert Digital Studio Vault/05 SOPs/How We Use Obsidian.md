# How We Use Obsidian

## Use Obsidian for
- notes
- research
- call prep
- strategy
- SOPs
- local market intel
- content planning

## Do not use Obsidian for
- live CRM state
- automated lead stage changes
- operational truth that already lives in the dashboard

## Source of truth split
- Dashboard / CRM = operational truth
- Obsidian = knowledge + context

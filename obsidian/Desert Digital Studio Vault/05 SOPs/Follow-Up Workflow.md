# Follow-Up Workflow

## Purpose
Keep DDS leads from going cold after the first outreach.

## Workflow
1. After outreach is sent, set a follow-up date in the CRM.
2. Review overdue follow-ups during morning checks/heartbeats.
3. Use a softer follow-up tone than the first message.
4. Keep follow-ups short, clear, and human.
5. Update stage/history in the CRM after each touch.

## Notes
- Prioritize leads that are reachable and still worth helping.
- Do not keep hammering polished businesses with weak opportunity.

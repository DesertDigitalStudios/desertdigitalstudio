# Cold Outreach Workflow

## Purpose
Turn scored DDS leads into actual conversations without sounding spammy.

## Workflow
1. Pick outreach-worthy leads, not just the highest raw score.
2. Prefer clean public email when available.
3. If no email exists, use Instagram/Facebook or phone/walk-in.
4. Draft outreach with a specific local angle when possible.
5. Do not send outbound messages without review unless clearly authorized.
6. Update the CRM after outreach is sent.

## Principles
- Specific beats generic.
- Local beats corporate.
- Reachable + meaningful problem beats polished-but-unreachable.
